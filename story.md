# Main Story

## Intro
Stranded on an island

## MI6 Headquarters
You get hired by MI6 because they're impressed by your escapade. Since they recently lost a valuable Agent X, you'll be replacing him. Welcome to MI6.

## 1st Mission: Container Ship
This was the mission that Agent X was working on when he was killed. He had almost brought the smuggler ring down, so there's some hints he left behind for you to use. The player finds out an
organization called VIPER was behind it.


## Last Mission: Infiltration
MI6 has found the leader of VIPER and sent you to kill him. You sneak into his house at night to kill him, but blah blah (Agent X is the head of VIPER; turns out MI6 is bad bc they're tryna
destabilize a third world country to get their oil).
; you go crazy and blow up MI6 headquarters)
<br>
The End: you get captured and taken away. You say "This isn't over yet". The screen cuts to black.
